# Support truths (owner of the support-retention role)

One file of the support surface, written and reviewed by the
Engineering: Product & DX + Customer: Support & retention lanes per the role
register (docs/agent-roles.md). It records what a paying customer can rely on;
a claim here ships only with a decision in docs/decisions.json.

- A plan expires at the end of its billing period; auto-renewal is off.
- A cancelled subscription keeps working until the period ends.
- Changing plan happens at https://navego.pages.dev/harga while signed in;
  Starter is $1/month, Basic $3, Plus $5, Pro $7, Advanced $9 in Asia/Arab/Africa
  and $1/$9/17/33 in Europe & the US.
- Refunds are opened from the payment provider and settle outside Navego.
