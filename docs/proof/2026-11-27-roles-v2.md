## The disk finding

While this work was in CI the C: drive hit **zero free bytes**. The session
snapshot cache had grown to **4.2 GB inside two minutes**, and a session that
cannot write its state stops without an error - which is the failure the owner
kept seeing when lanes died. Cleaning the npm cache, the old temp files and the
global snapshot cache brought the drive back to 7.3 GB free. The watchdog now
reads the free space every ten minutes and prunes the snapshot cache below the
floor, with a test that proves the prune and the no-op.
