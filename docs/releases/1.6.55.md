# WHAT REACHED THE SURFACE

The extension popup is rebuilt to the bar the W2G panel sets. One dark island ground, the Apex mark (a filled disc with the cursor knocked out of it, pointing NE), name and version under it, a gear door in the top row, a verdict panel with a single coloured dot and the reason under it, one full-width accent button, then quiet list rows — icon, title, one-line hint, no borders, no shadows, no glow. `#329` landed the island; `#331` finished it. The mark itself is the cursor-in-O from `#330`: the same geometry everywhere it appears — favicons, inline site marks, extension pages, and the four raster icons.

# WHAT CHANGED FOR CALLERS

- No tool moved; the surface stays 56 tools. Every clock reads 1.6.55.
- The popup's internal markup changed (`statusLine` is gone; `statusMain`/`statusSub` and the `.door` class carry the rows now); nothing else on the surface moves.
- The i18n tables grew `doors.*Hint` lines for the three navigational doors and the six `status.sub*` lines for the verdict row; `doors.settings` is new as well.

# WHAT IT COSTS THE READER

An update has to reach the machine that runs the reader's browser. Chrome and Edge force-install from the policy entries already on this machine; a load-unpacked copy does not self-update — reload the folder at `brave://extensions`, or restart that browser.
