# Rules the agent follows when it looks for customers

The agent may research the market and prepare a list of prospects. It may not send spam, and it may not make a purchase or a contract in the owner's name without the owner saying so.

## Why

FTC's CAN-SPAM guide covers every commercial email, business-to-business included. A message that advertises Navego needs accurate headers, a truthful subject, a clear statement that it is an ad, the sender's physical address, and a working opt-out that is honoured within ten business days. Each separate violating email can carry a penalty of up to $53.088. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

Google's generative-AI prohibited-use policy forbids spam, phishing, and tracking people without consent, and forbids automated decisions with a material detrimental impact in high-risk domains such as employment, housing, insurance, legal, healthcare, and finance without human supervision. Source: https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy

OpenAI's terms forbid automatically or programmatically extracting data or output, and forbid representing output as human-generated. Source: https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use

Anthropic's consumer terms forbid accessing the services through automated or non-human means unless Anthropic explicitly permits it. Source: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms

## The rules this loop runs on

1. Research is allowed: read public pages, read competitor pricing, read forums, record the finding with its source and date.
2. Preparing outreach is allowed: write the message and the list of recipients.
3. Sending bulk commercial email is not allowed without the owner's approval, the CAN-SPAM fields, and an opt-out that the system can honour.
4. Unsolicited automated contact through a portal is not allowed when the portal's terms forbid it.
5. Booking a meeting, accepting a contract, charging a card, or subscribing to a paid service is a consequential action and stays with the owner until the owner authorizes it in that act.
6. The agent may spend money from the economy wallet only for a subscription or API key that the owner set as a standing budget, and only inside the reserve the policy allows.
7. Money spent without money earned is minus, and the rule does not soften itself to make the loop look better.
