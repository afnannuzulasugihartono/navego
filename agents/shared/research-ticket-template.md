# Research ticket template

Research is not a lane. It is a bounded ticket written when a decision needs dated evidence.

Title: operator -> research task -> <date>

Body must name: the decision this evidence supports, the exact public URL to read, what fact to extract, the date of the reading, and the path where the result lands (docs/research/prospects.md or a dated proof file). Acceptance: one dated row with an openable URL, no invented claim, no personal contact data, and no outreach performed by the research task.

A research task never sends a message and never opens a standing agent.
