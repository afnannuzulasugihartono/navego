# Outreach policy

One bounded public action per day, recorded the same day in docs/proof/outreach/<date>.md with the surface URL, exact text, time and reply state.

Every message states what Navego is, the real price from the live pricing page, one concrete use case, an easy no, and the disclosure that the sender maintains Navego. Two negative replies stop a recipient; three refusals stop the lane until the owner says otherwise.

Never claim a testimonial, scarcity, a store review, revenue, customers or a trial that did not happen. Never use a personal address or a bought list.
