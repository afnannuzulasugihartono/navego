# Handoff from sales

When sales-rep records an affirmative reply, the operator writes a bounded onboarding ticket carrying the buyer's exact question and the proof block the buyer saw. Onboarding does not invent a new claim and does not contact the buyer directly unless that ticket says so.

The deliverable is a paste-ready first-win block and the driven evidence that it works. Money is claimed only from the settled payment record, never from the conversation.
